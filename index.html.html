Meet Arria Coburn

Nearly two decades of educational leadership rooted in student voice and equity

Magnet Schools of America, 2018

Dr. Arria Coburn is a Western Massachusetts educator with nearly 20 years of experience in schools. She began her career as a special education teacher before rising to assistant principal and then principal of the Springfield Renaissance School in Springfield, MA — a grade 6–12 magnet school where she led for over five years.

Under her leadership, Springfield Renaissance achieved a 100% college acceptance rate for ten consecutive years and outperformed the state on Massachusetts standardized assessments. The school was honored with the Magnet School of the Year award and received special recognition from the Massachusetts Department of Education for raising proficiency in both math and English.

Arria completed two fellowships in 2020–2021 — one with the Massachusetts Department of Education and another with Students at the Center. Her work is grounded in student voice, equitable access, and helping every young person see what's possible for their future.
